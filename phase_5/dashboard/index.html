✅ PBO
PBO (Probability of Backtest Overfitting) measures the likelihood that the best performing model configuration in-sample will perform worse than the median configuration out-of-sample. A PBO < 10% is generally considered robust.
📊

PBO & Strategy DNA

What this tells you: This score acts as a "Lie Detector" for your backtest.

✅ PBO < 10% (Skill): Your best model was chosen because it found real market patterns that repeat across different time periods.
⚠️ PBO > 40% (Luck): Your best model likely "got lucky" in the backtest and will probably fail when trading real money.
🛡️

Robustness & Outlier Test

Outlier Stress Test: We remove the top 5% of best returns. If the strategy becomes unprofitable, it relies too heavily on "lucky" spikes.
Impact Ratio: How much the top 5% of trades contribute to total profit. Lower is better for long-term reliability.
🌐

Market Regime Attribution

Volatility Regimes: High vs. Low volatility mean returns.
Directional Regimes: Performance during Bull (positive return bars) vs. Bear (negative return bars) contexts.
📈

Logits Distribution

What this graph tells you: It maps the "Distance from Luck."

👉 Hump to the Right (> 0): The strategy is Skill-Based. It consistently beats the average model across almost every data slice.
👉 Hump to the Left (< 0): The strategy is Overfitted. It only looks good on paper but fails when the data order changes.
🔴 The Red Line (0): The "Random Threshold." Anything to the right is proof of your bot's alpha.
