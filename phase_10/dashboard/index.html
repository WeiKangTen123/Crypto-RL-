Cointegrated Pairs Trading — Z-Score Mean Reversion

Concept: "The Rubber Band Effect"
This is a classic Statistical Arbitrage strategy. It finds two assets that historically move together (they are "cointegrated"). If one asset suddenly shoots up while the other stays flat, the "rubber band" between them stretches. The strategy bets that the rubber band will eventually snap back to its normal state.

It exploits the stationary spread between two log-prices that share a common stochastic trend. A long/short position is opened when the normalised spread deviates beyond ±2.5σ (maximum divergence) and closed as it reverts toward zero.

Example Scenario: BTC & ETH
  1. The Setup: BTC and ETH are cointegrated. The Z-score is floating around 0.
  2. The Anomaly: BTC pumps 8% while ETH only goes up 1%. The mathematical relationship is stretched. BTC is highly "overvalued" compared to ETH. The Z-score hits -2.8 (passing our -2.5 threshold).
  3. The Execution: We bet on them returning to equilibrium. We SHORT BTC (the overvalued asset) and LONG ETH (the undervalued asset).
  4. The Exit: Two days later, ETH catches up and BTC trades sideways. The Z-score returns to 0. We close both positions for a net profit.
① OLS Hedge Ratio (rolling window = OLS_WINDOW = 120 bars)
log(P_A[i]) = α + β × log(P_B[i]) + ε[i] β = hedge ratio (slope of OLS regression) α = intercept R² = coefficient of determination → must be ≥ MIN_R2 (0.80)
② Spread Construction
spread[i] = log(P_A[i]) − (α + β × log(P_B[i]))
③ Cointegration Test — Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF)
H₀: spread has a unit root (non-stationary) p-value < 0.05 → reject H₀ → spread is stationary → pair is cointegrated Computed over OLS_WINDOW bars; re-checked every bar (rolling regime filter).
④ Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Half-Life → Maximum Hold Period
Δspread[i] = λ × spread[i−1] + ε (OLS on consecutive spread differences) half_life = −log(2) / λ max_hold = half_life × 2 (trade must close within 2 mean-reversion cycles)
⑤ Z-Score Normalisation (rolling window = ZSCORE_WINDOW = 48 bars)
μ[i] = mean(spread[i−ZSCORE_WINDOW : i]) σ[i] = std (spread[i−ZSCORE_WINDOW : i]) Z[i] = (spread[i] − μ[i]) / σ[i]
⑥ Entry Gates (ALL must be true)
1. regime_ok — rolling ADF p-value < 0.05 on current window
2. circuit_ok — no circuit-breaker tripped (max daily loss not hit)
3. R² ≥ MIN_R2 — current window R² ≥ 0.80 (regression quality gate)
4. |Z| ≥ MIN_ENTRY_Z — |Z[i]| ≥ 2.5 (signal is large enough)
5. |Z| < Z_STOP — |Z[i]| < 8.0 (spread not already blown out)
6. CONFIRM_TURN — Z moved back toward zero by ≥1 tick from its peak
7. No open position on this pair
Direction: Z > 0 → short A / long B  |  Z < 0 → long A / short B
⑦ Position Sizing
capital_per_leg = CAPITAL × RISK_FRACTION / 2 size_A = capital_per_leg / P_A[entry] size_B = capital_per_leg / P_B[entry] × β (β-adjusted for delta-neutrality) fee_per_trade = 4 × capital_per_leg × TRADING_FEE (2 legs × open + close)
⑧ Exit Priority Ladder (checked every bar, highest priority first)
1. Z_STOP — |Z[i]| ≥ 8.0 — spread blew out, cut immediately
2. REVERSAL_TRAIL — Z crossed zero then re-diverged past trail threshold
3. MEAN_REVERT — |Z[i]| ≤ 0 — full mean reversion achieved
4. TIME_STOP — bars_held ≥ max_hold (= half_life × 2)
5. REVERSAL — Z reversed direction without reaching mean
⑨ P&L Calculation
gross_pnl = (exit_P_A − entry_P_A) × size_A × dir_A + (exit_P_B − entry_P_B) × size_B × dir_B (dir = +1 long, −1 short) fee = 4 × capital_per_leg × TRADING_FEE net_pnl = gross_pnl − fee ROI = net_pnl / (2 × capital_per_leg)
⑩ Optimised Parameters
Parameter Value Description
OLS_WINDOW 120 Bars for OLS regression & ADF test
ZSCORE_WINDOW 48 Bars for μ/σ normalisation
MIN_ENTRY_Z 2.5 Minimum |Z| to enter a position
Z_STOP_LOSS 8.0 |Z| at which position is force-closed
