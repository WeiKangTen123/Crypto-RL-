# RWA Momentum INJ

## Concept
Momentum following on tokens associated with Real-World Assets (RWA) which respect technical levels post-institutional announcements.

## Entry Conditions
- Consolidation on H1 or H4 charts.
- Breakout above resistance following an institutional RWA announcement.

## Exit Conditions
- Take profit: Resistance targets.
- Stop loss: Below consolidation support.

## Key Indicators
- Moving Averages (50, 200 EMA)
- Volume spike confirmation

## Expected Edge
RWA tokens respect technical levels better than retail meme coins, allowing for cleaner momentum trades.

## Data Requirements
- Columns needed: Close, Volume
- Minimum timeframe: 1h
- Minimum history: 2 years

## Implementation Notes
- Requires monitoring of specific RWA narrative-driven announcements.
