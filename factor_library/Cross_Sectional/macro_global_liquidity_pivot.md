## Strategy Name
Global Liquidity Pivot (M2 Lead Strategy)

## Category
Macro / Cross-Asset (DXY correlation, liquidity indices)

## Core Hypothesis — WHY should this strategy have an edge?
Bitcoin has evolved into the "fastest horse" in the race for global liquidity. Because it is a 24/7 global market with no central bank or circuit breakers, it reflects changes in the Global M2 Money Supply and U.S. Dollar liquidity (DXY) faster than traditional equities. In the 2024-2026 regime, BTC has shown a tendency to "lead" macro pivots by 3-6 months. When the Global Liquidity Index (GLI) bottoms and turns upward, BTC typically begins its impulsive leg before the Federal Reserve actually cuts rates.

## Entry Signal — exact condition that triggers a buy
**Long Entry:**
1. **Indicator:** Global Liquidity Index (Aggregated M2 from US, China, EU, Japan).
2. **Condition:** The GLI crosses above its 12-month Moving Average.
3. **Confluence:** DXY (US Dollar Index) is in a technical downtrend (below 200-day EMA).
4. **Trigger:** Monthly BTC candle closes above the previous month's high during a GLI expansion.

## Exit Signal — exact condition that triggers a sell or position close
1. **Target:** When GLI reaches a historical "over-expansion" level (Z-Score > 2 against its 2-year mean).
2. **Macro Conflict:** Exit if U.S. 10-Year Treasury Yields spike above 5% while Oil (WTI) is above $100 (signaling stagflation).
3. **Stop Loss:** 10% drawdown from monthly entry, or if GLI crosses back below its 12-month MA.

## Key Indicators / Data Required
- **Global M2 Money Supply:** Aggregated data (Central bank balance sheets).
- **DXY Index:** US Dollar strength.
- **GLI (Global Liquidity Index):** Often tracked by analysts like CrossBorder Capital or Raoul Pal.
- **US 10Y Yields & Oil Prices:** For stagflation regime filtering.

## Timeframe Compatibility (scalp / swing / position)
- **Primary:** Position (Multi-month to Yearly).
- **Strategy Type:** Regime Filtering / Macro Betting.

## Theoretical Risk — what market conditions break this strategy?
1. **Liquidity Trap:** If M2 is growing but the velocity of money is crashing, price may not follow liquidity.
2. **Regulatory Black Swans:** Sudden U.S. or Chinese bans on crypto access would decouple BTC from global liquidity momentarily.
