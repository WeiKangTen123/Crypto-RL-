## Strategy Name
Liquidity Sweep + Market Structure Shift (MSS)

## Category
Market Structure

## Core Hypothesis — WHY should this strategy have an edge?
Bitcoin is a highly manipulated market where "Smart Money" (institutional algorithms) often hunts retail stop-losses (liquidity) before initiating a real move. By waiting for a "Liquidity Sweep"—where price runs through a key level like the previous day's high or low—we are entering at the same time as institutions. The "Market Structure Shift" (MSS) acts as our confirmation that the manipulation is over and the real trend has begun, allowing us to enter with a tight stop-loss and high Reward-to-Risk ratio.

## Entry Signal — exact condition that triggers a buy
1. **The Sweep:** Price wicks through a major Liquidity Pool (e.g., Previous Day Low or Asian Session Low) on the 30m chart.
2. **The Shift:** On the 30m or 1h chart, price then rallies and closes above the most recent swing high (the "MSS").
3. **The Entry:** Price returns to retest the "displacement" area, specifically entering a 30m Fair Value Gap (FVG) or an Order Block created during the rally.

## Exit Signal — exact condition that triggers a sell or position close
1. **Target:** The opposite side's Liquidity Pool (e.g., if you bought a sweep of the Low, target the Previous Day High).
2. **Trailing Stop:** Move stop to break-even once price reaches 1:1 RR.
3. **Invalidation:** A close below the extreme low of the Liquidity Sweep.

## Key Indicators / Data Required
- **Previous Day High/Low (PDH/PDL)**
- **Asian Session Range (High/Low)**
- **Market Structure Shift (Swing Highs/Lows)**
- **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)**
- **30m / 1h OHLCV**

## Timeframe Compatibility (scalp / swing / position)
- **30m:** Primary signal timeframe.
- **1h:** HTF bias and structure confirmation.

## Theoretical Risk — what market conditions break this strategy?
"Expansion" regimes where the market doesn't reverse after a sweep but continues to trend aggressively. Also, "Low Volatility Chop" where structure shifts happen frequently but without follow-through.
