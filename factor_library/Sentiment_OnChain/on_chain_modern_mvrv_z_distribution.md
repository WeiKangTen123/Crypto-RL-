## Strategy Name
Modern MVRV-Z Distribution (Institutional Regime)

## Category
On-Chain Signals (MVRV, valuation bands)

## Core Hypothesis — WHY should this strategy have an edge?
The MVRV Z-Score measures the ratio between Market Cap and Realized Cap, traditionally used to identify cycle tops and bottoms. In the 2024-2026 "Institutional Era," the volatility of this metric has dampened. Institutional investors (via ETFs and corporate treasuries) tend to rebalance or take profits at lower valuation premiums than retail "moon-shot" speculators. Therefore, the historical "Red Zone" (Z-Score > 6) has become less relevant, and a new "Institutional Top Zone" (Z-Score 3.0 - 4.0) has emerged as the primary distribution area.

## Entry Signal — exact condition that triggers a buy
**Position Accumulation:**
1. **Indicator:** MVRV Z-Score.
2. **Condition:** Z-Score enters the "Green Zone" (below 0.5 but above -0.5).
3. **Requirement:** Must be accompanied by declining Exchange BTC Balances (confirming supply is being moved to cold storage/ETFs).

## Exit Signal — exact condition that triggers a sell or position close
**Position Distribution:**
1. **Partial Take-Profit (50%):** Z-Score reaches **3.0**.
2. **Full Exit / Hedge:** Z-Score reaches **3.5** or shows a daily divergence (Z-Score dropping while price makes a new high).
3. **Stop Loss:** If Z-Score drops below -0.5 (indicating a potential catastrophic break in realized value).

## Key Indicators / Data Required
- **MVRV Z-Score:** Market Value to Realized Value Z-Score (Glassnode, CryptoQuant, or LookIntoBitcoin).
- **Exchange Balances:** Total BTC held on all major exchanges.
- **Realized Cap:** The total value of all BTC at the price they last moved.

## Timeframe Compatibility (scalp / swing / position)
- **Primary:** Position (3 - 6 months).
- **Secondary:** Macro regime filter for other strategies.

## Theoretical Risk — what market conditions break this strategy?
1. **Hyper-Institutionalization:** If sovereign nations (Nation State adoption) begin buying, they may ignore MVRV valuation entirely, leading to a "super-cycle" that breaks the 3.5 Z-Score ceiling.
2. **Lost Coins:** High amounts of "zombie" coins moving after years can skew the Realized Cap suddenly, creating false Z-Score spikes.
