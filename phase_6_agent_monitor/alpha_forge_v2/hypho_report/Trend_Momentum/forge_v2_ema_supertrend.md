## Core Hypothesis — WHY should this strategy have an edge?

EMA crossovers identify the initiation of a new trend phase. Supertrend (ATR-based trailing stop from the EMA line) then defines "trend still alive" mathematically — the stop tightens as price rises (protecting gains) and widens during volatile consolidations (avoiding premature exits). The EMA entry + Supertrend exit is the purest implementation of "let winners run, cut losers short" in trend-following. The adaptive ATR basis means the system self-calibrates to crypto's wildly varying volatility regimes (2022 bear vs 2024 bull).
