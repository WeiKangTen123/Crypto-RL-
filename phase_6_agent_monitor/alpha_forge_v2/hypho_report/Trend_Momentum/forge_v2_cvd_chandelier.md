## Core Hypothesis — WHY should this strategy have an edge?

Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) is a running sum of (taker buy volume - taker sell volume). When CVD crosses from negative to positive, it signals that buy-side aggression has overwhelmed sell-side for the first time — a structural shift in order flow. This leading signal often precedes price breakouts by 1-3 bars because institutional accumulation shows in delta before price responds. Chandelier exit (ATR trailing from highest high) then captures the full trend leg while protecting against sudden reversals.
